Good to know

Where can I get frozen daiquiris to go in New Braunfels?

Sip Society + Daiquiri Depot at 311 FM 306, Suite 1A, New Braunfels, TX 78130 has a drive-thru for frozen daiquiris to go, from 16 oz cups to gallon jugs. A second location, The Backyard in Gruene, is at 1706 Hunter Rd.

How much is a gallon daiquiri?

A gallon jug of any daiquiri is $40, a half-gallon jug is $22, and the reusable River Jug is $18 with $12 refills. Single cups run $8.50 (16 oz), $10.50 (24 oz) and $13.50 (32 oz).

What hours is the daiquiri drive-thru open?

The FM 306 drive-thru is open Sunday–Thursday 9 AM–9 PM and Friday–Saturday 9 AM–10 PM.
