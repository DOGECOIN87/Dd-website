Made for the float

Pick any daiquiri flavor — or layer a few — and we'll pour it into a capped reusable jug you can bring back for $12 refills all season. The FM 306 drive-thru is on the corridor out to River Road, the Guadalupe and Canyon Lake, so you can stop on the way without a detour.

Know before you float

New Braunfels river rules ban disposable containers on the Comal and the city stretch of the Guadalupe, and glass and Styrofoam are out on Texas rivers generally — a reusable jug keeps you legal and keeps single-use cups out of the river. Alcohol rules vary by park and stretch, so check the rules for where you're putting in, and always float with a designated driver for the ride home.

Stocking up for the whole crew instead? Half-gallon and gallon jugs cover the cooler for the campsite, the lake house or the backyard.

Good to know

Can I take a daiquiri to the river in New Braunfels?

Drinks on the Comal and the city stretch of the Guadalupe must be in reusable containers — disposable containers are banned. The Daiquiri Depot River Jug is a reusable, capped jug made for river days; it is $18 filled and refills are $12.

Where do I get a River Jug?

At the Sip Society + Daiquiri Depot drive-thru, 311 FM 306, Suite 1A, New Braunfels — on the way to River Road, the Guadalupe River and Canyon Lake.
