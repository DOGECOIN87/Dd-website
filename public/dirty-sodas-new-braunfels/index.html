Dirty Sodas in New Braunfels

Your favorite soda, made dirty: cream, flavored syrups and a squeeze of something good, over ice. No alcohol — just the good stuff — at the FM 306 drive-thru and on the lawn at The Backyard in Gruene.

The designated driver's favorite stop

Dirty sodas are the pour for anyone skipping alcohol without skipping the fun — same stop as the daiquiris, so the whole car orders in one pass. Round it out with boba milk teas and no-alcohol smoothies for the kids and the DD.

Two places to get one

  • The Depot — 311 FM 306, Suite 1A. Drive-thru, Sun–Thu 9 AM–9 PM, Fri–Sat 9 AM–10 PM.
  • The Backyard in Gruene — 1706 Hunter Rd. Turf lawn, live music, big screens. Hours change with events — check Facebook or call ahead.

Good to know

Where can I get a dirty soda in New Braunfels?

Sip Society + Daiquiri Depot pours dirty sodas at 311 FM 306, Suite 1A (drive-thru) and at The Backyard in Gruene, 1706 Hunter Rd, New Braunfels.

No — dirty sodas are soda with cream and flavored syrups, no alcohol.
