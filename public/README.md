# Public assets

These files are business-provided assets.

Brand:

- `brand/sip-society-daiquiri-depot.webp`: the Sip Society + Daiquiri depot logo (navbar, footer, and the print on the 3D cups)
- `brand/backyard-in-gruene.webp`: The Backyard in Gruene round guitar logo
- `brand/now-in-2-locations.webp`: "Now in 2 locations" social graphic

3D models:

- `models/gallon-jug.glb`: "Milk 1 gallon jug from USA" by boost5u (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/milk-1-gallon-jug-from-usa-7e64723df1854c5b8d45419fe86d96b5), CC BY 4.0, slimmed and smoothed by `scripts/slim-gallon-glb.py`. The licence requires credit; the owner removed the footer line, so the credit lives in the file's asset metadata (keep it there).

Photos:

- `photos/fm306-drive-thru.webp`: the FM 306 storefront and drive-thru (FM 306 card), colour-graded to the site palette with `scripts/grade-photo.py`
- `photos/backyard-in-gruene-night.webp`: The Backyard in Gruene at night (Gruene card)
- `photos/layered-daiquiris.webp`: layered and swirled daiquiris at FM 306 (social share image)
- `photos/jugs-and-jello-shots.webp`: half-gallon jugs with jello shots and gummy bears (not currently used)

Only add images the owner has approved, and avoid AI-generated imagery. Run bright, saturated photos through `scripts/grade-photo.py` so they sit with the site's cream and pink palette.

Favicon and legacy monogram files are also here.

Videos are not in `public/`. They live in `/media/video`, so they are not deployed. They are AI-generated ads and should not be put on the site as-is.

Please replace the web-sized assets with full-resolution originals if they become available.
